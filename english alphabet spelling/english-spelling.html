📋 How to Use

  • Click individual letter boxes to select them for editing
  • Use voice recognition for hands-free spelling
  • Say letters individually: "A", "S", "K" (not "ASK")
  • Use the permanent letter buttons (B,D,P,T,M,N) for quick access
  • Orange buttons (B,D,P,T) and purple buttons (M,N) for commonly confused letters
  • Use utility buttons to clear, undo, or get hints
  • Check your spelling accuracy with the Check button
  • Practice with different words using New Word
